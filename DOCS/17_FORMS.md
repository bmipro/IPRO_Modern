# Forms

## What It Is

A way to build a custom form once — with text fields, multi-line text, checkboxes, dropdowns, and section headers — and reuse it on any page of your website with a **Custom Form** block. Visitor submissions land in your Website Leads inbox, the same place contact-form and newsletter signups already go.

## Build a Form

1. Select **Forms** in the Agent Portal menu (under Website).
2. Click **New Form**.
3. Enter a form title (shown to visitors), an optional description, and the submit button text and success message a visitor sees after submitting.
4. Click **Add Field** for each question or input you need, and choose its type:
   - **Text (single line)** — a short answer, e.g. a name or reference number.
   - **Text (multi-line)** — a longer answer, e.g. "tell us more."
   - **Checkboxes (select multiple)** — a group of options a visitor can pick any number of, e.g. "which services are you interested in?"
   - **Dropdown (select one)** — a single choice from a list.
   - **Section header** — a heading with no input of its own, used to break a long form into clearly labeled parts.
5. For Checkbox and Dropdown fields, add 2 or more answer options.
6. Mark a field **Required** if a visitor must fill it in before submitting (not available on Section headers).
7. Click **Save Form**.

A form is fully editable and deletable at any time — editing a form updates it everywhere it's currently attached.

## Attach a Form to a Page

1. Open the page in the Website Builder and click **Add Block**, then choose **Custom Form**.
2. In the block's settings, pick which form to display from the dropdown.
3. Save the block. Visitors to that page now see the form, always showing whatever fields the form currently has — no need to touch the page again if you later add or change a field.

The same form can be attached to more than one page or block.

## Send a Form Through a Campaign

A form doesn't need to be on a page to be sent out — from a Campaign's **Details** screen, add a step from the **Forms** tab and pick one of your forms. This adds an email step with a link to that form, hosted on your own site, that clients can click straight from their inbox. Your site needs to be published for the link to work, since the form page lives on your site's own domain.

## What Visitors See

Every Custom Form block always asks for a first name and email address (required), plus an optional phone number, above your own custom fields — this is what lets a submission become a real lead in your CRM. The form includes the same spam protections as every other public form on your site (a hidden honeypot field, a timing check, and a simple math question).

## Where Submissions Go

Each submission creates a Website Lead of type **Form submission**, with a summary of every answer shown right in the Website Leads list. Click **View Answers** on a form-submission lead to see each field and its answer laid out individually — useful when a visitor selected several checkboxes at once.

## Deleting a Form

Deleting a form doesn't delete any leads it already generated — their submitted answers are kept exactly as they were received. Any page still displaying the deleted form's block simply stops showing that block; nothing breaks.
