# Agent Document Library

## What It Is

A private place for an agent to store their own business documents — marketing materials, compliance forms, personal templates — separate from any specific client's record. This is different from **Portal Documents**, which live on an individual client's record and can be shared with that client.

## Upload a Document

1. Select **Documents** in the Agent Portal menu.
2. Choose a file (PDF, Word, Excel, image, TXT, or CSV — max 20 MB per file).
3. Optionally enter a category (e.g. "Marketing", "Compliance") to help organize and filter later.
4. Click **Upload**.

## Find a Document

1. Type part of a file name in the search box, choose a category in the **All categories** list, or both.
2. Click **Filter**. The list shows only the matching documents.
3. Click **Clear** to show every document again.

## Download or Delete

- Click **Download** to retrieve a document.
- Click the trash icon to permanently delete a document. This also removes the underlying file from storage.

## Storage Limit

Every package includes this feature, but each package has its own total storage limit. The
numbers below are the standard defaults -- your own limit is whatever your package currently
grants, and the Documents page always shows your real usage and limit:

| Package | Storage limit |
|---|---|
| Silver | 50 MB |
| Gold | 500 MB |
| Platinum | 1000 MB |
| Broker | 1000 MB per user: you and each active team member (3000 MB with two team members) |

The limit is one total for your account, and everything you store counts toward it. On a package
whose storage says "per user", that total is the per-user figure for you and once more for each
active team member; the Documents page says how many people it counts. Deactivating a team member
takes their share off the total (nothing is deleted, but uploads stop while you are over it):

- documents in this library;
- your website's images, including the Image Library and gallery photos;
- article images;
- files you and your clients upload to the client portal.

The **Documents** page shows **Storage used** with a bar (e.g. "142 MB of 500 MB"). If an upload would exceed the limit, it's rejected with a message showing current usage — delete unused documents or images to free up space, or contact support to discuss more storage.
