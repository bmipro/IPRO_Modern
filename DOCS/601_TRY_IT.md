# Try it: Google Calendar times (601)

What changed: follow-ups now land in your Google Calendar on the right day and at the right time. Before,
an appointment showed 4 hours early (5 hours in winter), and a follow-up with only a date showed at 8 PM the
evening before.

Before you start: in your portal open **Account > Profile**. Check that **Time Zone** is Eastern Time and that
**Calendar Source** shows your Google account connected. Google Calendar's own time zone should be Eastern
too. iPro and Google talk every 15 minutes, so after each step wait up to 15 minutes, then refresh. iPro's
Follow-up Calendar shows days, not hours.

1. **The old ones put themselves right.** In Google Calendar, look at the follow-ups iPro put there before
   this update (from the last 30 days onward). Within 15 minutes of the update they move by themselves: one
   that sat at 8 PM the evening before its date is now an all-day item on its date, and an appointment that
   sat 4 hours early is at its real time. In iPro nothing moves.
2. **A follow-up with only a date.** Open a client and, under **Follow-ups**, add "Test 601 date" due this
   coming Thursday. In Google you should see an all-day item on Thursday (not Wednesday evening).
3. **An appointment before the clocks go back.** In **Portal Requests**, schedule a client's appointment
   request for a weekday before Sunday 1 November at 2:00 PM. (No request waiting? Sign in to your client
   portal as a test client and ask for one first.) In Google: 2:00 PM to 3:00 PM on that day.
4. **An appointment after the clocks go back.** Schedule another one for a day after 1 November at 10:00 AM.
   In Google: 10:00 AM on that day, not 9:00 or 11:00.
5. **Move one in Google.** In Google, drag the 2:00 PM appointment to 9:00 PM on the same day. In iPro's
   Follow-up Calendar it stays on that same day (before this update it would have jumped to the next day).
   Your test client's portal, under **Appointments**, now shows 9:00 PM.
6. **Move the all-day one.** In Google, drag "Test 601 date" to Friday. In iPro it moves to Friday.
7. **One of your own Google events.** In Google, add "Test 601 dinner" at 9:00 PM on any day. In iPro's
   Follow-up Calendar it appears in grey on that same day (before this update, on the next day).
8. **Clean up.** Delete the test follow-ups in iPro (that removes them from Google too) and the dinner in
   Google.

If something looks wrong, note the step number, what Google showed and what iPro showed.
