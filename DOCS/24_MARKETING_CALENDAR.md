# Marketing Calendar

## What It Is

A month-at-a-glance view of your outbound marketing: newsletters, drip campaign steps and social posts, on the day they went out or are scheduled to go out. It is for seeing the shape of your month, not for creating things.

## Open It

Select **Marketing Calendar** in the Agent Portal menu (under Marketing). It opens on the current month with today outlined, and the heading says how many marketing events the month holds.

## Move Around

- The two buttons at the top move to the previous or next month; they are labelled with the month names.
- Each day cell holds a coloured tile per event:
  - **Newsletter**, showing the subject line.
  - **Social**, showing the post topic.
  - **Campaign**, showing "campaign name: sent to N".
- The legend at the bottom maps the colours.

## Jump to an Item

Click a tile to open the item it stands for: a newsletter opens in the newsletter editor, a social post in the post editor, a campaign step on the campaign's Details page.

The three buttons in the header, **Newsletter**, **Campaigns** and **Social Posts**, take you to those areas to create something new.

## What Appears and When

- **Newsletters** appear on their sent date, or on their scheduled date if they have not gone out yet, so a scheduled newsletter shows in the future.
- **Social posts** appear when they have a "Schedule for" date or a posted date.
- **Drip campaign steps** appear only after they have actually been sent. Future steps are not forecast on the calendar; see the campaign's Details page for its schedule.

## What Is Not Here

- E-Cards, E-Letters, Polls and Did You Know emails do not appear on this calendar. Their delivery is under **Email Activity**.
- Client follow-ups and appointments are on the client **Calendar**, not here.
- There is no week or day view, no filter by type, and nothing can be dragged or created by clicking a day.
- Days are placed by calendar date without a time-zone adjustment, so an event sent very close to midnight can show on the neighbouring day.

## Package Availability

The Marketing Calendar is available on every package.
