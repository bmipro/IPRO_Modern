# AI Daily Assistant

## What It Is

Every night the platform reviews your website leads and follow-ups and writes one prioritised **Suggested next action** onto your dashboard, with a short AI-written sentence on why it matters. It is a nudge for the first thing to do in the morning, not a report.

## Where It Shows

The **AI Daily Assistant** card sits at the top of the Dashboard, directly under the welcome line.

## Read the Card

1. Three counters: **New leads**, **Leads over 24 hours old**, **No follow-up scheduled**.
2. **Suggested next action**, one of:
   - call a named client because a named follow-up is N days overdue;
   - call a named lead that has been waiting N hours;
   - schedule a follow-up with a named client because nothing is on the books;
   - **You're all caught up** when none of those apply.
3. A grey one-line explanation from the AI of why that action matters, when one was generated.
4. **View** opens the right place: the client's record, the new-leads inbox, or the follow-up's client.

The priority is fixed: an overdue follow-up beats a waiting lead, which beats a client with no follow-up.

## How Often It Updates

Once a day, overnight. There is no refresh, regenerate, dismiss or snooze button. If you have already dealt with the item by the time you look, the card checks the live record and falls back to "all caught up" rather than showing stale advice.

## What Is Sent to the AI

Only a one-line description of the situation, with no client name, email or identifier, for example "a client follow-up task is 3 days overdue". One thing to know: for an overdue follow-up, the **title you typed on the follow-up** is included in that line. Keep client-identifying detail out of follow-up titles if that matters to you. When nothing is due, no AI call is made at all.

If the AI explanation could not be generated, the counters and the suggested action still appear; only the grey sentence is missing.

## Package Availability

The AI Daily Assistant is included with **Platinum** and **Broker** packages. On other packages the card shows the upgrade message instead.
