# Team Member Logins

## What It Is

Give your assistant or office staff their own login to your account. A team member sees and works with the same clients, newsletters, website and everything else you do, under your account. Two things stay yours alone: **Billing** and **My Team** itself, so a team member can never change your subscription or add more logins.

## Seats

Each package includes a number of team member seats. The **My Team** page shows how many are in use, for example "1 of 2 seats in use". A deactivated member does not use a seat. If your package includes no seats, the page says so and offers the packages.

## Add a Team Member

1. Select **My Team** in the Agent Portal menu (under your account).
2. Enter the person's **Full name** and **Email**. The email is their login and must not be used by any other login on the platform.
3. Click **Add**.
4. A **temporary password** is shown once, in a yellow box at the top of the page. It is never emailed. Copy it and pass it to the person directly, by phone or in person.

The new member now signs in at the regular login page with their email address and the temporary password.

## Their First Login

At first login the person must choose their own password (at least 8 characters) before they can go anywhere else. Until they have done that, their row on My Team shows a **Temp password** badge. Once they have signed in with their own password, the badge disappears and **Last login** shows when they were last in.

## Reset a Password

If a team member forgets their password, click the key icon on their row. A new temporary password is shown once, the same way as when you added them, and they must choose a new password at their next login.

## Deactivate, Reactivate, or Remove

- **Deactivate** stops the person from signing in and frees their seat. Their row stays, so you can **Reactivate** them later, provided a seat is free.
- **Remove** deletes the login for good. It asks you to confirm.

Nothing the person did is lost by deactivating or removing them: clients, notes, newsletters and pages belong to your account, not to the login.

## What a Team Member Cannot Do

- Open **Billing** or **My Team**. Those pages send them back to the Dashboard.
- Sign in with your email address. Every login has its own email.

Everything else, including changing their own password under Account, is open to them.
