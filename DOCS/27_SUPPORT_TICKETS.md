# Support and Help

## What It Is

The Support page is two things: the **Help Documentation** library you are reading now, and **My Tickets**, a ticket desk for reaching platform support. A ticket is a conversation that stays inside the portal; email only tells you when something changed.

## Open It

Select **Support** in the Agent Portal menu. The help articles are at the top; your tickets are below.

## Read a Help Article

Click **Read article** on any tile. Articles cover each area of the product, from your account and clients through newsletters, the website builder, domains, billing, the client portal and more.

## Raise a Ticket

1. Click **New Ticket**.
2. Enter a **Subject** (a short summary) and describe the issue in the box below it. Both are required.
3. Click submit. You see "Your ticket was submitted. We'll respond as soon as possible." and the ticket opens.

Support is notified straight away.

## Follow a Ticket

- **My Tickets** lists your tickets, newest activity first, with filters **All, Open, In Progress, Resolved, Closed**.
- A red **New reply** badge on a row means support has answered since you last opened it. Opening the ticket clears it.
- Inside a ticket, messages run oldest to newest; support's messages are highlighted and labelled "(Support)".
- Type in the **Send Reply** box to continue the conversation. Replying to a resolved or closed ticket reopens it.
- Click **Mark Resolved** when your question is answered. Only support can close a ticket for good.

## What the Statuses Mean

| status | meaning |
|---|---|
| Open | waiting for support |
| In Progress | support has replied and the ticket is being worked |
| Resolved | you, or support, marked it answered; reply to reopen it |
| Closed | closed by support |

## The Emails You Receive

When support replies you get an email with the reply, an **Open your ticket** button, and a note that reads "Please do not reply to this email". Replies to that email are not read; continue in the portal, either through the button or by signing in and opening **Support > My Tickets**.

## What Is Not Here

Tickets have no attachments, categories or priority levels today. To share a screenshot, describe where it is or upload it to your Documents and mention the file name in the ticket.
