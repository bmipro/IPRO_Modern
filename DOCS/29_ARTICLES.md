# Articles

## What It Is

A library of reusable content you write once: a title, a short summary, the full text and an optional cover image. The same article can then appear on your website (blog listing, a dedicated article page, the Did You Know teaser), be inserted into a newsletter, or become a drip campaign step.

## Write an Article

1. Select **Articles** in the Agent Portal menu (under Marketing) and click **New Article**.
2. Optional: under **Draft with AI**, type a topic and click **Draft with AI**. It fills the title, summary and content for you to review; nothing is saved until you save.
3. Enter the **Title**.
4. Enter the **Summary**, up to 300 characters. This is the teaser line shown in the blog listing and on the Did You Know block.
5. Write the **Content** in the editor.
6. Optional: add an **Image** (JPG, PNG, GIF or WebP, up to 8 MB). This is the cover used by the blog listing, the Article Page block and newsletter inserts.
7. Tick **Published** to make it available publicly, then **Save Article**.

Edit an article at any time from the list. On the edit page you can replace the cover, or tick **Remove this image** to drop it.

## Where an Article Can Go

- **Your website blog.** Add a **Blog** block to a page: it shows your newest published articles automatically, newest first. Settings: how many posts to show, and whether to show cover images. A visitor's **Read more** opens the full post on the same page.
- **A dedicated page.** Add an **Article Page** block, choose the article under **Which article?**, and the page shows its image, title and full content.
- **Did You Know.** Tick the article in a **Did You Know Teaser** block; visitors see the summary and can request the full article by email (see that guide).
- **A newsletter.** In the newsletter editor, under **Insert from Articles**, choose the article and click **Insert**. This copies the title, content and image into the newsletter as an article card.
- **A drip campaign.** Add or replace a campaign step from an article; the title becomes the subject and the content the body.

Inserting into a newsletter or a campaign makes a **copy**. Editing the library article later does not change newsletters already built or steps already created. Editing a published article does change your website immediately.

## The Starter Articles

New sites come with a **Resources** section built from starter articles for your business type, plus two general ones ("How to Get the Most Out of Your First Meeting" and "Questions Worth Asking Before You Choose an Advisor"). They are real articles in your library, published, each with its own page. Edit or delete them like anything you wrote yourself.

## Draft or Published

Only published articles appear on the website or in Did You Know. Unticking **Published** removes an article from every block that shows it, immediately and without a placeholder. The date shown on the blog is the date the article was first published.

## Limits and Availability

- Cover images are limited to 8 MB and count toward the storage pool you share with Documents and website images.
- **Delete** on the Articles list is permanent and asks no confirmation. Unpublish instead if you may want the article back.
- Articles need the Newsletters feature in your package. **Draft with AI** needs the AI Assistant feature, included with Platinum and Broker.
