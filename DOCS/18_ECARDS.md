# E-Cards

## What It Is

A quick way to send a client a pre-designed occasion card by email — Happy Birthday, Thank You, Season's Greetings, or Congratulations — with a short personal message overlaid on the card and your own contact card (name, designation, company, phone, cell, fax, email, website, photo) attached underneath. No template to design or image to upload — the card is built automatically from your own accent color and profile info.

## Send an E-Card

1. Select **E-Cards** in the Agent Portal menu (under Marketing).
2. Click **Send an E-Card**.
3. Pick an occasion — the live preview on the right updates as you choose.
4. Enter an email subject and, optionally, a short personal message. The message shows directly on the card itself, so keep it brief.
5. Select which clients should receive it. Only clients with an email address on file can be selected — use the search box to filter a long list.
6. Choose **Send now** or **Schedule for later** (uses your profile's time zone).
7. Click **Send E-Card**.

## What Recipients See

An email with the occasion card at the top — your accent color blended into a per-occasion color, the occasion headline, and your typed message — followed by your contact card. There's no tracking pixel or click-through link beyond your own reply-to address; a reply goes straight to your inbox.

## Notes

- Your **accent color** (Agent Portal → **My Website** → **Portal Accent Color**, the same one used on Newsletter emails) sets the card's base color. Set one there before sending your first e-card if you want a specific brand color.
- Your **photo, designation, company, phone, fax, cell, email, and domain** come from your Profile — update them there, not per e-card.
- Sending is per-recipient (like Newsletter), so one bounced or invalid address never blocks the rest of the list.
- Scheduled e-cards are picked up within a minute of their scheduled time by the same background dispatcher that sends Newsletters and Did You Know follow-ups.
