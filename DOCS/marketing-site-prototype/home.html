Straight answers

Four things we don't do.

We don't send text messages.

Reminders go by email. SMS is on the list. It isn't built, and we're not going to tell you it is.

We don't touch your clients' money.

You can invoice a client through IPRO and they can pay online, but it goes to your payment link, not through us.

We don't post to social media for you.

You can draft a post here. You still publish it.

We don't have a phone app.

The whole thing works in a browser on your phone, which is a different claim, and the honest one.
