# Testimonials

## What It Is

A way to collect client testimonials directly from your website and choose which ones appear publicly. A visitor fills out a short form on your site; you review what they wrote, edit it if needed, and approve or reject it. Nothing is ever shown on your site until you approve it.

## Add the Form to Your Website

1. Go to **My Website** > edit a page (or create one, e.g. a page called "Testimonials").
2. Choose **Testimonial Submission Form** beside **Add Block**, then click **Add Block**.
3. Set a heading and any supporting text, then **Save Block**.
4. Publish the page.

The block renders the submission form. Once you've approved one or more testimonials, they appear in a list underneath the form on that same page — nobody sees a submission until you approve it.

Some business types/packages may already include a starter "Testimonials" page with this block pre-added; you can still add the block to any other page yourself.

## What a Visitor Sees and Submits

The public form asks for first name, last name, email, and the testimonial text. It's protected by the same spam defenses used on your contact form (hidden honeypot field, a minimum-fill-time check, and a simple math question) — genuine visitors filling it out normally are never affected.

## Review Submissions

1. Select **Testimonials** in the Agent Portal menu (under Website).
2. New submissions land under the **Pending** tab.
3. Click **Edit** to read the full text and adjust wording (fix typos, tone down anything inappropriate) before approving.
4. Click **Approve** to publish it to your site, or **Reject** to keep it hidden without deleting it.
5. Use the **Approved** / **Rejected** / **All** tabs to see everything you've reviewed. Click the trash icon to permanently delete a submission.

Approving or rejecting doesn't require re-editing first — you can act directly from the Pending list, or open **Edit** first if you want to adjust the wording.

## Request a Testimonial from a Specific Client

Instead of waiting for a visitor to find the form on your site, you can ask one client directly:

1. Open the client's profile from **Clients**.
2. Under **Testimonial**, click **Request Testimonial**.
3. The client receives an email with a personal link to a short feedback form (just their testimonial and a consent checkbox — no captcha, since the link itself is only ever sent to that one person).
4. Once they submit, it lands in your **Pending** queue exactly like an open-form submission, ready to edit and approve.

The client profile card shows the current state — awaiting response, submitted and awaiting your review, or the outcome once you've approved or rejected it — with a **Resend Request** or **Request Another** button as appropriate. Clicking the button again before they've responded resends the same link rather than creating a duplicate request.

## Ask a Client for a Google Review

A Google review helps people who search for you find you. iPro can send a client your Google review link:

1. Copy your Google review link (see **Where to find your Google review link** below).
2. Open **My Profile**, paste it under **Google Reviews** in **Your Google review link**, and make sure **Let me ask clients for Google reviews** is ticked. Click **Save Profile**.
3. Open the client's profile from **Clients**. Under **Testimonial**, click **Ask for a Google Review**.

The client receives a short letter from you with a **Leave a Google review** button that opens Google's review box for your business. Replies come to your profile email. The client's card then shows when you last asked; a second request to the same client within a day is not sent.

A client who writes you a testimonial through **Request Testimonial** is also offered your Google review link on the thank-you page, and your website's **Reviews** blocks use it for their "Review us on Google" link when they have none of their own.

**Where to find your Google review link.** On a computer, sign in to the Google account that manages your business and search Google for your business name; the panel for managing your business shows at the top. Click **Ask for reviews** (on some screens **Read reviews**, then **Get more reviews**) and copy the link it shows, which looks like `https://g.page/r/.../review`. In the Google Maps app the same link is under **Customers** > **Reviews** > **Get more reviews**. No link yet? Your business needs a Google Business Profile first: claim it at business.google.com.

**Financial and insurance advisers:** this starts switched off for you. Your dealer's or regulator's rules on reviews and testimonials come first, so tick **Let me ask clients for Google reviews** only if they allow it.

Nothing is sent automatically, and a client who has unsubscribed from your emails is never sent one.

## Package Availability

Testimonials is included with every package. If a package removes access to it later, agents keep any testimonials already approved and visible on their site, but can no longer add new **Testimonial Submission Form** blocks or reach the review queue until they upgrade.
